## Mathematical Models ##

**Binding** <br>
Model 1 
$$
\frac{dB}{dt} = k_{on} \cdot N \cdot R - k_{off} \cdot B
$$
Model 2 
$$
\frac{dB}{dt} = k_{on} \cdot N \cdot (R - B) - k_{off} \cdot B
$$

**Endocytosis**
$$
\frac{dI}{dt} = k_{e} \cdot B
$$

**Cell Clearance**
$$
\frac{dS}{dt} = -k_{kill} \cdot I \cdot S
$$
